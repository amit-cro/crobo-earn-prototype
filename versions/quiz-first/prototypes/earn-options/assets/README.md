# Editorial prototype imagery

Created with the built-in image generation tool for the isolated Quiz-first prototype. These are original illustrative photographs, not customers or testimonials. Generated PNGs were converted to JPEG at quality 85 for the web. No source photographs were retouched.

## Files

- `coastal-morning.jpg`: homepage hero and managed portfolio panel, 1536 × 1024.
- `long-horizon.jpg`: Growth panel and supporting homepage landscape, 1024 × 1536.
- `cash-morning.jpg`: Cash panel, 1536 × 1024. Generated with the built-in image tool; converted to JPEG at quality 85.

## Generation prompts

### Cash morning

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial background photo for a cash interest account card in a premium personal finance website. Create one high-quality landscape 3:2 photograph of a peaceful sunlit seaside terrace, viewed through open French doors, a light linen curtain moving in the breeze along the right edge, a small pale stone table with a simple ceramic coffee cup at lower right, calm blue sea and a distant coast in the background. Feeling: freedom, easy everyday living, time to yourself. Authentic lifestyle photography, fine subtle film grain, airy soft blue, sea-glass green and warm cream palette. Composition: clear pale blue sky and very low visual detail across upper left half for large dark typography which will be added in code; terrace and detail concentrated right and lower third. No people, no text, no numbers, no logo, no graphics, no borders.

### Coastal morning

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a premium editorial photograph for a financial wellbeing website hero. Wide landscape composition, 3:2. A young adult South Asian woman with softly windblown dark brown hair, wearing an ivory linen shirt, enjoying a quiet sunny moment looking slightly upward toward the right, eyes open, natural relaxed expression. She is positioned in the rightmost third of the composition, shown from chest upward, bottom edge crops her torso. The entire left half is clean open rich sky blue with very subtle clouds and atmospheric grain, usable for large white typography added later. Background a clear coastal blue sky with soft luminous haze on right. Refined film photography, tactile subtle grain, warm natural sun on skin, sophisticated magazine art direction, realistic anatomy, candid rather than corporate stock. Broad quiet palette of azure blue, pale blue, white, warm skin. No typography, no logos, no UI, no watermark, no money symbols. The subject must not touch the left half. Output image suitable for an actual website asset.

### Long horizon

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: tall premium editorial landscape photograph for a financial wellbeing product card. Vertical 2:3 composition. Rolling green coastal hills in Big Sur, California, early morning mist in the valleys, luminous pale blue sky with faint warm peach sunrise near the horizon. Foreground wild grasses and a few delicate small white wildflowers; layered hills create natural depth. Understated fine film grain, beautiful natural photographic light, realistic not fantasy, refined travel magazine photography. Keep the upper third airy and uncluttered to accommodate interface labels added in code; middle has soft mist and rolling landscape. No people, text, logos, coins, graphs, symbols or UI.
